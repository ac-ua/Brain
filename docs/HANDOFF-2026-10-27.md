## Decisions taken with Aurélien (blueprint v0.5)
1. **Architecture.** Notion holds knowledge and state, `ac-ua/brain` holds the harness, any AI can execute. Formats: AGENTS.md, SKILL.md, MCP.
2. **Agents.** Capability × domain model. Phase 1 has 3 agents (chief-of-staff, builder, content-studio), built from existing know-how.
3. **Notion structure.**
   - No separate "cloud brain": the structure is integrated into his existing Notion.
   - A System section (About me, Memories, Session Log, Brain Inbox), plus Home, Tasks, Projects and Build Roadmap.
   - One page per domain, made from a template, created when that domain gets migrated.
4. **Facts vs ways of working.** Facts go to Notion right away. Ways of working go to the Brain Inbox. A weekly routine turns the inbox into a PR on `brain`. The brain has its own roadmap, version number, changelog and a monthly retro.
5. **Roadmaps.** One shared database with per-project views. A project shared outside, or with specific fields, gets its own database from the same template.
6. **Notion backup.** A manual native export once a month (active pages only) to the personal Drive. The archive is exported once, then left out. No script for now.
7. **Where work runs.**
   - Pro work runs locally on the work Mac.
   - Personal work runs in a cloud session.
   - Writing to the personal Drive from the cloud goes through rclone in `perso-general` (to test).
   - Never move pro files to personal storage.
8. **Cloud environments.** `app-element` and `app-base-camp` (each with its own secrets), plus `perso-general` (the default, no app secrets). Aurélien split them on 2026-10-27.
9. **Claude Code, Projects and Cowork.** Claude Code is the main workshop. Projects and Cowork stay for quick conversations. The content of existing Projects migrates into the brain when its domain gets touched.
10. **Autonomy.** No send, publishing or payment without Aurélien. Deletions need his approval. Drafts, Notion and commits are free. This can be relaxed later, domain by domain (for example automated prospecting).
11. **UA data and plugin.**
    - UnifyApps data stays in his Notion.
    - The UA plugin is copied to personal storage once a month (it is also his own work).
    - Every two weeks, a review takes generic lessons from pro to personal, with no client names.
12. **Voice clone.** Only on explicit request. The code may later go to a separate private repo `ac-ua/voice-clone`, without the recordings. Nothing about the clone goes in `brain`.
13. **Content.** Short-form first (TikTok and Instagram, several posts a week, possibly one a day on TikTok), published by hand at first. The strategy is content-studio's first job. Around 200 GB of footage sits on the personal Drive.
14. **Other decisions.**
    - Sprinklr gets archived as is.
    - The Spanish app is an artifact for now and will move to a repo.
    - Free plans are the default.
    - Aurélien is fine editing personal content on the work Mac.
    - DaVinci Resolve is also on his iPad.
