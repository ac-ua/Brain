# interview-prep

Personal and confidential (domain Career): nothing goes to work systems, the work Drive or work Slack, and the work
Mac's pro folders are not used. **Single source: the master profile artifact**
(https://claude.ai/artifact/DgwrKLFR7H5pdhDLv4cf9v, see `domains/career.md`): read it first, never trim it; new
facts Aurélien confirms are added to it (on his OK) and dated.

## 1. Brief (ask once)
Company, role, interviewer(s) and their role, date and format, stage, what he wants from this role, the job
description and any recruiter notes. Read the Career page in Notion for earlier rounds with this company.

## 2. Research
The company's product, market, stage, funding, recent news, the team, the interviewer's background (public
sources only, dated, linked); what the role really needs; 3 ways his experience maps to it (from the profile, with
the proof: systems he built, numbers).

## 3. The pack
| Piece | Content |
|---|---|
| **Interview brief** | the company in 10 lines, the role, why him (3 proofs), what to learn from them, his asks |
| **Pre-mortem** | "the interview went badly: why?" → 6–10 risks (gap, doubt, red flag, weak story), each with the answer and the proof |
| **Cheat sheet** | one page: pitch in 60 s, 3 stories (situation → what he built → result), numbers, questions to ask, closing line |
| **Answer drill** | the 10 likely questions, his answer in his voice (direct, no hedging, "the systems I built"), then a live drill when he asks ("drill me": one question at a time, feedback after each) |
| **Prep podcast** (on request) | `explainer-video`, stock voices, one listener: him; longer is fine (20–30 min) |

Artifacts or a Notion page under Career, as he prefers; never in a work folder.

## 4. Forms and questionnaires
Prefill from the profile and earlier filings; give paste-ready text per field (`message-drafting` patterns), and a
`.docx` when the form is a document. When a description must reconcile his real role with an earlier one (visa,
green card), keep it truthful and consistent, and say what was reconciled. Legal choices are his and his lawyer's:
flag, don't decide.

## 5. After
Debrief in five lines (questions asked, what landed, what didn't, next step, date) → Notion Career page; tasks with
dates → Tasks (Domain Career); a thank-you note draft (`message-drafting`) if he wants one.
