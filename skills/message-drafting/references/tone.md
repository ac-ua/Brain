# Aurélien's tone

Corrections he made to drafts (Sep–Oct 2026), as rules. Add new ones through the Brain Inbox.

- **Too long / too short → in between.** He tunes length by feel; offer the middle, not a third extreme.
- **Remove hedging.** Replace vague or modest phrasing with what he did ("systèmes que j'ai construits").
- **Sharper = shorter and the ask first.** A note to a leader about a bottleneck led with the problem and offered
  to write the requirements.
- **Give him the text, don't edit their doc.** For shared documents and forms: "tell me what to paste where".
- **Same style as an earlier paragraph** means the same length and structure, not the same words.
- **Close client emails on a proposed slot** (day and time), modelled on his last email to that client.
- **Explaining a process to a team in Slack:** simple and short, a few lines, the cadence and who does what.
- **Answers to forms (visa, legal, recruiters):** consistent with his master profile and with earlier filings; when
  his real role and an earlier description differ, reconcile them and say so.

## Register
- Direct, friendly, no corporate filler. Emojis rarely, never in client emails.
- French or English as the thread is; UnifyApps channels default to English.
