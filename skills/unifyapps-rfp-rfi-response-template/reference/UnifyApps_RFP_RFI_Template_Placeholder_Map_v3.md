## Overview

This is the master template. The five placeholders represent the AI-generated sections; everything from **Deployment and Security Model** onward is standard content, including the embedded **UnifyApps Platform Capabilities** appendix (A.1–A.6) and a table of contents already wired to all standard sections including that appendix. See `UnifyApps_RFP_RFI_Template_Content_Instructions_v3.md` and the shared `UnifyApps_RFP_RFI_Template_Content_Instructions.md`.

## Replacement Rule

Each placeholder represents one full content section. Replace the entire placeholder with fully generated content for that section. Do not insert separate micro-placeholders. Do not rewrite the standard section starting from **Deployment and Security Model** through the end of the document, including the embedded Platform Capabilities appendix.

## Placeholders

| Placeholder | Location | Replace With | Expected Content Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| `{{DOCUMENT_TITLE}}` | Cover page | Customer-specific document title | Short title only |
| `{{EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY}}` | Executive summary section | Concise executive summary | 4-7 short paragraphs |
| `{{OVERVIEW_PROPOSED_SOLUTION}}` | Overview Proposed Solution section | Proposed solution narrative, objectives, components, and alignment | Narrative + tables |
| `{{UNDERSTANDING_OF_REQUIREMENTS}}` | Understanding of Requirements section | Detailed understanding of customer requirements, current state, target state, requirement interpretation, assumptions, and success factors | Detailed narrative + requirement mapping tables |
