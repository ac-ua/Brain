# message-drafting

Aurélien sends; Claude drafts. Never send, post or schedule a message (needs Aurélien every time). Tone samples and
patterns: [`references/tone.md`](references/tone.md).

## 1. Frame (ask nothing you can infer)
Who reads it (client exec, AE, team lead, colleague, recruiter, lawyer), the channel (email, Slack, form field), the
one thing the reader must do or know, the language (his message's language, else the reader's), and the length.
His own earlier message, when given, is the model: same length, structure and register.

## 2. Draft
- **Lead with the point** (the ask, the decision, the answer) in the first line.
- **Short**: Slack 2–6 lines; client recap = 3–5 bullets + one proposed slot; form answers = the exact text for
  each field. Tune the length to the request ("in between" means between his two last versions).
- **Concrete, not hedged**: "the systems I built", not "I may have contributed to"; numbers and dates over
  adjectives; no "just", "I think maybe", "hope this helps".
- **Nothing he didn't say**: no new commitment, metric, price or date. Unsure → a `[à confirmer]` marker.
- **His register**: direct and warm; English for UnifyApps channels unless the thread is in French.
- Personal and pro never mix: a pro message carries nothing from his other domains, and the reverse.

## 3. Deliver
The message in a code block (paste-ready), then at most two lines: what changed vs his draft, and one alternative
if the tone is a judgement call. For forms: one block per field, labelled with the field name.

## Patterns
| Ask | Shape |
|---|---|
| Client recap ("what we heard") | thanks in one line → 3–5 bullets of what we heard → what we'll bring next → a proposed slot |
| Internal Slack (status, ask) | the ask or the news → 2–3 lines of why → who does what by when |
| Explain a process simply | 3 steps max, one line each, a link for details |
| Sharper version | same content, half the words, the ask first |
| "Same style as X" | mirror X's length, opening, structure and closing |
| Form or questionnaire | exact text per field, consistent with his profile and earlier filings; flag any conflict |
| Reply to a team thread | answer the question asked first, then context |

Long-form writing (decks, docs, case for change) is not this skill: `case-for-change`, `work-notes`.
