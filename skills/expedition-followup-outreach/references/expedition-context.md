## The Expedition (core facts)

- **Route:** Hercules Inlet → South Pole, solo, unsupported, unassisted
- **Distance:** ~1,130 km (~702 miles)
- **Duration:** ~50 days
- **Departure:** November 2026
- **Conditions:** -14°C to -50°C, wind, complete isolation

## Strategic Notes by Category

**Portable power (Anker, Goal Zero, Jackery, EcoFlow, Bluetti):**
Lead with "product validation in extreme conditions" — -50°C is the ultimate 
stress test. Frame as a technical challenge, not an adventure story.

**Nutrition (Chomps, Naak, Lyophilisé & Co, Feastables):**
Lead with consumption context — 50 days alone, 6,000+ kcal/day, -50°C palatability.

**Tech/content (GoPro, DJI, Insta360):**
Lead with content creation angle — the footage is the product validation and 
the content asset simultaneously.

**Apparel (Icebreaker, Compressport, Decathlon/Simond):**
Lead with product validation. Note: compression garments are contraindicated 
at -50°C — pitch Compressport for transit, in-tent recovery, and pre/post phases only.

**Eyewear (Julbo):**
UV + whiteout conditions. Very specific technical need at altitude/latitude.

**Watch brands (Tudor, Rolex):**
Extreme environment narrative. Do not pitch as sponsorship — pitch as 
"expérience partenaire" or documentary integration.

**Airlines (Air France):**
Logistics angle — Paris → Punta Arenas routing. Content partnership possible 
for pre/post expedition storytelling.

**Financial/luxury (Mastercard, Amex, Visa, BNP WM, SG PB):**
Never "sponsorship". Frame as exclusive experience partnership, content rights, 
or client storytelling asset.

**Mapping (ZeroSixZero):**
Likely a paid engagement. Explore whether a tech sponsor could fund this.

**Tracking (Polarsteps, FindPenguins):**
Platform content partnership — live expedition tracking as a product showcase.
