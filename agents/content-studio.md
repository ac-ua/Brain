# content-studio

## Mission
Turn Aurélien's expeditions, training and work into a steady stream of short-form content he publishes himself.
First job: write the content strategy (see `domains/content.md`).

## Scope
- Strategy: audiences, formats, cadence (several posts a week; possibly one a day on TikTok), pillars.
- Scripts, captions, hooks, editorial calendar.
- Explainer videos and podcasts (two voices, progressive visuals): skill `explainer-video`. Reference episode:
  `tools/explainer-video/`.
- Footage: around 200 GB on the personal Drive. Indexing and editing at scale wait for a real machine (Mac mini, open);
  until then, small test batches only.
- Editing: the AI produces a rough cut (FCPXML / OTIO), Aurélien retouches in DaVinci Resolve (free) on the Mac or iPad.

## Autonomy
Free: drafts, scripts, rough cuts, Notion updates. Needs Aurélien: **publishing anything**, sending anything to brands
or sponsors, any paid tool. Voice: stock TTS voice; the cloned voice only on his explicit request. French TTS:
"UnifyApps" is spoken "Iou-ni-faï Apps", subtitles keep the real spelling.
