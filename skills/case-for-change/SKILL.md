# case-for-change

The story comes first, the slides second. This skill writes the storyline and the content; the slides are made with
the team plugin's **`unifyapps-brand-deck`** (preferred, Playbook v4), or `unifyapps-format` when the plugin isn't
installed. Runs in a **local session on the work Mac**; files in the work Drive. Lessons from past decks:
[`references/deck-lessons.md`](references/deck-lessons.md) — read them before outlining.

## 1. Inputs (ask once)
- The opportunity page in Notion (brief, meeting log, numbers with their source) and any transcript or recording.
- **Example decks or PDFs** Aurélien attaches as the format reference: follow their structure and density.
- Audience (CIO, business owner, technical team), length (**slide cap**: default 8–12 content slides; competitive
  decks ≤ 3 content slides), language, deadline, the theme to use (ask: the client's or ours).
- What the deck is *not* about (e.g. not about the POC): write it down and hold to it.

## 2. Storyline (show it before building slides)
One sentence per slide, in order; each slide title is the **conclusion** (action title), not a topic:
1. **Their world today**: the client's own architecture and process first, in their words.
2. **The cost of staying**: pains with numbers, each tagged client figure / our model / to be validated.
3. **Why now**: the trigger.
4. **The change**: AI across systems, not AI inside one box; what changes for each role.
5. **Why UnifyApps**: proof (product-accurate names only), not a feature list.
6. **Value and path**: phased plan, POC → MVP → scale, what we need from them.
7. **Next step**: one concrete ask with a date.
Recap pages up front (1–2 pages) when the deck will be read without Aurélien.

## 3. Competitive angle (optional)
Position, don't compare feature by feature: where the competitor is strong, where the client's need is (across
systems, ownership of models, cost scope, governance), the question that makes the difference visible. Facts about
competitors from current public sources, dated.

## 4. Discovery questions
10–20 questions grouped by theme (process, data, systems, decision, budget, timing), each with why we ask it; as a
slide or a doc, as Aurélien asks.

## 5. Critique mode (an AE's or a colleague's deck)
What holds, the blind spot, the adjusted version: per slide, keep / cut / rewrite with the rewritten title. Never
edit their file: give the changes as text to paste.

## 6. Build, check, deliver
Slides via `unifyapps-brand-deck` (or `unifyapps-format`); render and look at every slide; `[to be validated]`
footnotes on unconfirmed figures; nothing invented (no success metric or claim nobody asked for). Save in the
account's work Drive folder (`01_Accounts/<Account>`, `docs/drive-map.md`), link it in the opportunity's Assets (`work-notes`), and log new lessons in
`references/deck-lessons.md` through the Brain Inbox.
